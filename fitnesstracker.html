The Workout Studio

Created by Peyton Ingram, Donye Richardson, and Jeremy Nelson, designed the user interface using Canva to ensure a clean and user-friendly experience. The app provides a comprehensive platform for managing fitness and nutrition goals, class enrollments, and payments. Members can update profiles, securely pay fees, enroll or unenroll in classes, and track fitness progress with reminders. It offers personalized meal plans, dietary logging, virtual personal training, and administrator tools for managing schedules and announcements. Designed for high availability, security, and scalability, the app supports up to 10,000 concurrent users, integrates with external devices, runs on iOS and Android, complies with privacy regulations, and includes accessibility features to serve users of all abilities.
